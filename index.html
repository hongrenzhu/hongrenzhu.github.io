About

I’m a Master of Statistics student at the University of Hong Kong. I graduated from Dalhousie University in Halifax in 2025 with a first-class honours BSc in Statistics, then spent a gap year as a data analyst intern working on expressway safety in Shandong. Most of my work involves data indexed by time or place: accidents along a road, biodiversity in a marine protected area, and a few image classifiers.

Internships

Shandong Yimeng Traffic Development Investment Group

Research

Jan – Jun 2025

Loss functions and optimizers on MNIST

Research paper

Trained convolutional networks in PyTorch under nine loss–optimizer combinations with the other hyperparameters held fixed, then compared accuracy, F1, convergence and training stability. Focal loss with SGD came out on top at 99.16% test accuracy and an F1 of 0.9916.

Nov 2024 – May 2025

Comparing neural network architectures on CIFAR-10

Honours thesis, Dalhousie University

Implemented five architectures in PyTorch, with data augmentation and GPU training, and compared their accuracy, convergence and resource use. ResNet was the most accurate at 93.68%; the CNN was the most parameter-efficient.

Sep 2024 – Jan 2025

Biodiversity in the Musquash Marine Protected Area

Research project, Dalhousie University

Built biodiversity indices from Fisheries and Oceans Canada data for 2010–2023 and modelled their seasonal and spatial variation with linear regression, generalised additive models and regression trees. Sediment texture and organic content explained the most.
